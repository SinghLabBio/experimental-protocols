# Protein electrophoresis
Grab a gel (we use Criterion mini 12% 15wells 15 ul). Remove comb from the top and precint from the bottom and put it on the holder in the electrophoresis chamber. Fill chamber with Tris-Glycine-SDS buffer until previously precinted gap and the top of the gel  are covered. 
Load desired volume on each well. Tipically 5ul of pure protein or 15ul of cell lysate. Set constant voltage at 250mV and timer at 25mins.
Remove from the chamber, crack the gel plastic and...

## Commassie staining
Put gel on a tray and add Bulldog coommassie staining solution. Gentle shake for 30 mins. Remove staining and immerse the gel in water

## Western Blotting
Fill electrophoresis chamber with Tris-Glycine-20%ethanol buffer. Set Black side of the sandwich on the botom, put an sponge and a filter paper on it and immerse on same buffer. put the gel on the paper and, then, the nitrocellulose membrane NO BUBBLES BETWEEN THEM. put another filter paper, another sponge and close the sandwich. Insert on WB cassete and run for 2h 25mA.
Remove membrane and immerse on TBST-5%BSA buffer. gentle shake for 1h. Remove buffer and add fresh buffer with antibody (1:10.000 for pure protein, 1:500 for cell lysates). Gently shake over night at 4C.
Remove antibody and wash with TBST 3x 5min gently shaking. Add secondary antibodies (concentration 1/2 of primary in TBST-5%BSA) and incubate 1h room temperature gently shaking.
Wash again and develope. We do LiCor system. Put membrane upside down on the screen, select size to adapt to your membrane and hit Intensity - Auto Quality - High.
